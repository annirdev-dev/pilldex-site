Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

  • Your medicines stay on your phone. What you add to Pilldex (people, medicines, doses, counts, identified pills) is stored only on your device. We can’t see it.
  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell or share your information.
  • Photos are read on your phone. Pictures you take to identify or count pills are not uploaded.
  • One thing leaves your phone: when the app shows an official pill photo, it downloads it from our image storage, which sees a normal web request (see below).

Who we are

Pilldex (“the app”) is made by Annir Dev (“we”, “us”). The app is for users in the United States. Questions or requests: annirdev@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your first name, if you enter one, and the names of family members you add.
  • Medicines: name, strength, pills per dose, times and days, start and end dates, pills left, and how the pill looks.
  • Your dose log (taken or skipped), pill counts, and the pills you identified.
  • How many photo scans and counts you used today (for the free plan’s daily limit).
  • Settings, such as light or dark appearance.

Pill searches run on a database built into the app, so the imprints, colors and shapes you type are not sent anywhere.

Like other app data, this information is included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups if you use them. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

Camera and photos

When you scan or count pills, the app uses your camera or a photo you pick. The photo is analyzed on your phone using Apple’s built-in text recognition and our counting method. It is not uploaded, and the app does not add it to your photo library. A temporary copy can stay in the app’s cache until iOS clears it. When you pick a photo, iOS only gives the app the photo you choose, not your whole library.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. No push service is used. A reminder shows the medicine’s name and, for family members, the person’s name (for example “Mom: Lisinopril”). Depending on your settings, it can appear on your lock screen. To hide the text, go to Settings › Notifications › Pilldex › Show Previews.

What leaves your phone

Official pill photos

Official pill photos are stored with our hosting provider Firebase Hosting, a service of Google (Google LLC, United States; photos are delivered from Google’s servers worldwide). When the app shows one, your phone downloads it like any web image. To deliver it, Google receives your IP address, basic technical details of the request (such as the app and iOS version) and the photo’s file name, which identifies the pill. Google keeps such technical logs for a short time for security and operations. We don’t use them to identify you, don’t combine them with other information, and only look at them to fix technical problems.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. We don’t receive your payment details, name or Apple Account email. On your phone, Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchases.

Links and calls you start

If you open a drug’s full label, it loads from DailyMed (dailymed.nlm.nih.gov, U.S. National Library of Medicine). “Manage subscription” opens Apple’s subscription page. Support and legal pages open from our website, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The emergency buttons start a phone call to Poison Help or 911. Those services handle your information under their own policies.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites, and the app never asks for permission to track.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising.
  • We don’t use your location.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We delete support emails when they are no longer needed, and at the latest after 2 years.

Health information and HIPAA

Pilldex is a consumer app. We are not a health care provider, health plan or business associate, so HIPAA does not apply to the app. That is one reason the app keeps your medicine information on your phone. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for what U.S. state health-data laws require.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can add a child’s medicines; that information stays on the parent’s phone. If you believe a child sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a medicine or a family member in the app at any time.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app. If you use backups, older backups can still contain it until they are replaced.
  • Turn off reminders, camera or photo access in iOS Settings.
  • Cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Security

The app’s data sits in its private area on your phone, where iOS keeps other apps out. When your iPhone is locked with a passcode, iOS encrypts it. Please use a passcode, especially if you store family members’ medicines.

Your privacy rights in the U.S.

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have rights such as knowing what personal information a business holds, getting a copy, correcting it and deleting it. Because we hold almost nothing about you (at most the short-lived technical logs above and emails you send us), most requests can be answered quickly. You can still make a request at annirdev@gmail.com, or through an authorized agent. We answer within 45 days, may need to confirm your request is genuine, and won’t treat you differently for using your rights. We don’t sell or share personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we don’t use sensitive personal information for any purpose other than running the app.

If you are in the EU or UK

Annir Dev is based in the European Union, so the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the little personal data we handle. We are the controller of the technical photo-request logs and of emails you send us. We process the logs because we have a legitimate interest in delivering photos securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and emails to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Google processes the logs on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing, and complain to a data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects what data leaves your phone, we’ll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
