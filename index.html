Identifying pills

How do I identify a pill?

Open Identify and type the letters and numbers printed or pressed into the pill. Spaces don’t matter. If both sides have an imprint, add the back side too. Color and shape are optional: they only sort the matches. Open a match to compare your pill with the official photo, size and score line.

Why is there no match, or the wrong one?
  • Check look-alike characters: 0/O, 1/I/L, 5/S, 8/B. Try one side only and leave out logos.
  • Pills from other countries, many supplements and some older pills aren’t in U.S. label data.
  • A pill with no match could be counterfeit. Don’t take it; ask a pharmacist.
How does scanning with the camera work?

Take a photo of the pill on a plain surface, close up and in good light. The app reads the imprint on your phone and fills it in for you to check. Pressed-in imprints are harder to read than printed ones, so always check the text. The free plan includes 3 photo scans a day; typing is always unlimited.

Counting pills

How do I get an accurate count?
  • Use a plain surface that contrasts with the pills: a dark tray for white pills.
  • Spread the pills so they don’t overlap, and hold the phone straight above them.
  • Tap a missed pill to add it, and tap a number to remove a wrong one. Always double-check.

The free plan includes 3 counts a day.

Medicines and reminders

My reminders don’t arrive
  • Settings › Notifications › Pillmark: Allow Notifications must be on.
  • Check that a Focus mode (such as Sleep) isn’t silencing Pillmark.
  • Open the app once after restarting your phone or updating iOS.
What do the four boxes on Today mean?

Like a pill organizer, the day has four boxes: Morning, Midday, Evening and Night. A filled box still has pills to take; tap it when they’re taken. Hold a box to take, skip or undo each dose on its own. Only today can be changed.

How do I add my family?

Family members are part of Pillmark Family. Go to Me › Family › Add, then add medicines for each person. Every medicine and reminder shows whose it is.

Subscription

What does Pillmark Family cost and include?

It adds family members, unlimited photo scans and counts, and your full medicine history. The price and any free trial are shown in the app before you subscribe. Typing an imprint, safety help and your own medicines are always free.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Pillmark › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period.

I bought it but don’t see it on my new phone

Open Me › Pillmark Family and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. We can’t see your medicines, family or history. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your data moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone. Restore the backup on the new phone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete medicines or people in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Older backups can still contain it until they are replaced.
