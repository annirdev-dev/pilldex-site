Identifying pills

How do I identify a pill?

Open Identify and type the letters and numbers printed or pressed into the pill. Spaces don’t matter. If both sides have an imprint, add the back side too. Color and shape are optional: they only sort the matches. Open a match to compare your pill with the official photo, size and score line.

Why is there no match, or the wrong one?
  • Check look-alike characters: 0/O, 1/I/L, 5/S, 8/B. Try one side only and leave out logos.
  • Pills from other countries, many supplements and some older pills aren’t in U.S. label data.
  • A pill with no match could be counterfeit. Don’t take it; ask a pharmacist.
How does scanning with the camera work?

Take a photo of the pill on a plain surface, close up and in good light. The app reads the imprint on your phone and fills it in for you to check. Pressed-in imprints are harder to read than printed ones, so always check the text.

Counting pills

How do I get an accurate count?
  • Use a plain surface that contrasts with the pills: a dark tray for white pills.
  • Spread the pills so they don’t overlap, and hold the phone straight above them.
  • Tap a missed pill to add it, and tap a number to remove a wrong one. Always double-check.

Medicines and reminders

My reminders don’t arrive
  • Settings › Notifications › Pilldex: Allow Notifications must be on.
  • Check that a Focus mode (such as Sleep) isn’t silencing Pilldex.
  • Medicines with a start date or a set number of days get reminders up to 30 days ahead. Open Pilldex at least once a month so the next ones are set.
What do the four boxes on Today mean?

Like a pill organizer, the day has four boxes: Morning, Midday, Evening and Night. A filled box still has pills to take; tap it when they’re taken. Hold a box to take, skip or undo each dose on its own. Only today can be changed.

How do I add my family, or people I care for?

Looking after others is part of Pilldex Family. Go to Me › Family (or People you care for) › Add, then add medicines for each person. Every medicine and reminder shows whose it is. It works for family carers and home carers; it is not an official medication record for care homes.

Subscription

What does Pilldex Family cost and include?

Pilldex Family unlocks everything: medicines and reminders for you and everyone you look after, looking up, scanning and counting pills, and your full history. New subscribers can try it free for 7 days; the price and the trial are shown in the app before you subscribe. Without a subscription you can still open the app, see your schedule and use emergency help, and reminders you already set keep arriving.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Pilldex › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period.

I bought it but don’t see it on my new phone

Open Me › Pilldex Family and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data and account

Do I need an account?

No. You can continue as a guest and keep everything on your phone. Sign in with Apple (on the last intro page, or in Me) if you want a backup, your data on a new phone, or to share with family.

Where is my information stored?

As a guest, only on your phone. If you sign in, also in your Pilldex account, stored with Google Cloud in the U.S. Only you and people you invite can see it. Photos you take are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you signed in, install Pilldex on the new iPhone and sign in with the same Apple Account: everything comes back. As a guest, your data moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

How do I delete my data or my account?

Delete medicines or people in the app, or delete the app to remove what is on your phone. If you signed in, go to Me › Account › Delete account to delete your account and everything saved in it. Older phone backups can still contain data until they are replaced.
